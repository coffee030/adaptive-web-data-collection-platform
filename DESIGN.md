# Harbourline interface

Marketing Intelligence organizes the workspace around a campaign brief, published creator evidence and the decision to save, compare or request prices. The overview offers searches supported by the current catalog and the latest work from each eligible source. Creator discovery keeps search terms, filters and sorting in the URL. Source links, credits, dates and permissions sit beside the work they describe. Reports separate observed company results from editable scenarios.

The two workspaces share the Tabler-derived navigation, panels, forms and table primitives in the studio layer. Marketing uses indigo for actions and source-specific colors for platforms. Operations uses teal for actions, amber for attention and a distinct error treatment. Both use tabular numerals, English and Traditional Chinese font fallbacks, labelled inputs, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support. [Shared workspace components](frontend/src/components/studio/Workspace.tsx) · [Marketing styles](frontend/src/features/marketing/business.css) · [Operations styles](frontend/src/features/operator/frame.css)

On a phone, users can open navigation and filtering independently. Search results lead into the same profile, comparison and campaign views available on desktop. Wide analytical tables retain named, scrollable regions. Operators can follow a publication through to its collection job, validation result and evidence. Signed-out deep links explain their destination and offer the appropriate recovery action.
