# M10 Real Website Feasibility

Assessment date: **2026-11-16** (Hong Kong). Both decisions concern the approved URLs and the current M10 Core. The network probes were bounded feasibility requests. Live Core collection, Evidence and exports were **NOT RUN** for both sites.

| Website and proposed topic | Decision | Decisive finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FTC · Advertising, Endorsements and Influencer Disclosures | **UNSUPPORTED** | Robots forbids the proposed `/search/` path. An anonymous request to the permitted Disclosures 101 detail URL returned HTTP 403 in this environment. |
| New York Fed · Financial Markets and Economic News | **UNSUPPORTED** | The public site advertises a GET search form, but its documented parameter returned HTTP 403 from the evaluation environment. The Core requires a compatible keyword flow for public HTTPS. |

## Federal Trade Commission

- The website policy permits reuse of most FTC-authored government material with source attribution where feasible. Verify content-specific rights for third-party material, preserve attribution to the United States Federal Trade Commission and avoid implying FTC endorsement or using its seal. The policy describes content use. It does not grant access to restricted or blocked endpoints. [Website policy](https://www.ftc.gov/policy-notices/website-policy)
- The wildcard group in robots specifies a five-second crawl delay. It disallows `/search/`, `/index.php/search/`, login/admin paths and query patterns that include `combine` or `items_per_page`. Those paths and query patterns were excluded from testing. The public endorsement guidance and linked Disclosures 101 article are under `/business-guidance/`, outside the retrieved disallow rules. [Robots policy](https://www.ftc.gov/robots.txt) · [Guidance listing](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews)
- The official guidance listing presents links to document URLs, including a stable path for Disclosures 101. A bounded direct request returned **403** (451 bytes). A search parameter, server-rendered search result structure, pagination and a Core-compatible permitted alternate search could not be verified. No authentication or CAPTCHA workaround was attempted. The editorial content is about disclosure requirements. The Synthetic Marketing preset illustrates fictional partnership disclosures. [Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
- At five-second spacing, robots + two result pages + four details would require at least 30 seconds before response durations. The keyword runner can apply robots timing within its 45-second maximum and 20-request cap. Timing alone cannot make a disallowed search compatible. A single public HTTPS document cannot be run through the Core's direct HTML entry point.

## Federal Reserve Bank of New York

- The Terms permit access by automated means where it does not damage or interfere with the website. Retain the original headline, copyright notice, source identifiers, author and source URL, and follow the required attribution format and any content-specific restrictions. Label modifications and preserve the Terms on distribution. Permissioned services, third-party content and research materials can have additional conditions. [Terms of Use](https://www.newyorkfed.org/privacy/termsofuse.html)
- A single direct GET to the official robots file returned **200**. The wildcard group had no crawl-delay or request-rate directive and disallowed `/PrincipleInterest/` and two research-calendar PDFs. The evaluated `/search`, `/research/search.html` and `/markets/op_policies` paths were outside those rules. A conservative candidate run would start with one page, one detail, one attempt and a deliberately low request rate, all within the Core budget. [Robots policy](https://www.newyorkfed.org/robots.txt)
- Initial research-page HTML exposed a separate global form using `GET /search` and the `text` parameter. One anonymous GET to `/search?text=market` returned **403** (381 bytes). Evaluation stopped on the block. Result selectors, client-side rendering requirements, same-origin detail links and pagination behind that endpoint remain unverified. The legacy research-publication form uses `keyword` and other fields and documents abstract/detail pages that may link to PDFs. Its submission endpoint and a compatible static GET result were unverified. [Research search form](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/search.html) · [Research search instructions](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/search_tips.html)
- A response header probe for `/markets/op_policies` returned **200**, and the official listing exposes dated links to market statements. Stable article paths are candidates for identity after a successful compatible search. The listing and its detail pages describe economic news and policy operations. Individual stock quotes are outside this evaluation. [Market statements](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/op_policies) · [Markets and Policy Implementation](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets)

## Opt-in decision

Neither website passed the gate for the current approved HTTPS workflow. The launcher has no live collection action for these sites. If a later evaluation verifies an allowed and compatible endpoint, the operator must explicitly authorize its Source, validate and approve its immutable Profile, approve a topic binding and submit a bounded request. Verify actual Worker output, stored Evidence, attribution, collection time and exports before labelling the results `REAL WEBSITE DATA`. Use an independently labelled topic and keep the existing Core HTTPS, destination, robots and block handling in place.
